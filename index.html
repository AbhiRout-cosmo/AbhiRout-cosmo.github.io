Abhishek Rout

Theoretical physicist · Ph.D. in Physics, University of South Carolina (2025)

I study how gravity behaves in its most extreme regimes, where our understanding of spacetime starts to break down. My current focus is parity violation in gravity and the signatures it leaves on black holes, gravitational waves and the early universe.

Research

Themes

I work analytically wherever possible and use Python and Mathematica for the parts that need numerics and visualisation. I'm also interested in the conceptual and philosophical side of physics, and in science fiction that takes spacetime seriously.

Chern–Simons modified gravity

Black hole perturbation theory

Axial perturbations, quasinormal modes and isospectrality breaking of charged and rotating black holes in dynamical Chern–Simons gravity.

Bound states and particle production in time-dependent backgrounds, including breather-type field configurations.

Gravitational-wave phenomenology

Observable signatures of parity violation: birefringence of primordial gravitational waves and parity-violating strain from pulsar glitches.
